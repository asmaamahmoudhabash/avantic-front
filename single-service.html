Research & Development

Pioneering the Future of Medicine

At Avantic Pharma, our Research & Development (R&D) division is the beating heart of our innovation engine. We are dedicated to discovering, developing, and delivering transformative medicines that address severe unmet medical needs. Our multidisciplinary teams of scientists work tirelessly utilizing advanced analytical platforms and state-of-the-art laboratory facilities.

We provide end-to-end R&D services spanning from early stage drug discovery to late-stage formulation development, scaling up, and preparation for commercial manufacturing.

Formulation Development

Creating robust solid, liquid, and semi-solid dosage forms ensuring maximum stability and bioavailability.

Analytical Testing

Extensive analytical method development and validation complying strictly with global ICH guidelines.

Our R&D Process

Initial assessment of API characteristics, solubility profiling, and compatibility studies to select the optimal excipients for robust product design.

Developing multiple batches using Design of Experiments (DoE) methodology to pinpoint the optimal combination that yields maximum efficacy.

Transitioning lab-scale models successfully to pilot batches and finally into full commercial manufacturing sizes without loss of quality.
