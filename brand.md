# Avantic Brand Guidelines

## Colors
- Primary (Brilliant Azure): #29ABE2
- Dark Blue (Yale Blue): #1F4E8C
- Medium Blue (Button Blue): #2196C4
- Light Accent (Spiro): #33CCFF
- Background: #f0f8ff
- White: #ffffff
- Text Dark: #1F4E8C

## Typography
- English Font: Ping LT (use Inter or Poppins as fallback)
- Arabic Font: Ping AR + LT (use Cairo or Tajawal as fallback)
- Headings: Bold, uppercase
- Body: Regular / Medium weight

## Brand Name
- English: AVANTIC
- Arabic: أڤانتك

## Slogan
- English: "Science Forward, Healthier World"
- Arabic: "العلم للأمام، عالم أكثر صحة"

## Logo
- Path: assets/images/logo.png
- Use on white or #29ABE2 blue backgrounds only

## Visual Style
- Clean, modern, medical/pharma
- Blue gradient backgrounds
- Bubble and molecule visual elements
- Minimalist with bold typography
